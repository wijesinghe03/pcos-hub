Cookie Policy

Transparent information about how we use cookies to provide a secure and efficient healthcare experience.

Last Updated: March 4, 2025

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit most websites. They help websites remember you, your preferences, and your session information.

At PCOS Care Hub, we use cookies to ensure that our platform remains secure, efficient, and personalized for your health journey.

Our platform does NOT use intrusive tracking cookies or sell your browsing behavior to third-party advertisers. Our cookies are focused purely on platform functionality and security.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following essential purposes:

Category Purpose
Authentication To keep you signed in while you navigate between the dashboard, reports, and tracking tools.
Security To protect your sensitive health data and prevent unauthorized access to your account.
Preferences To remember your language settings and custom dashboard layout choices.
Performance To understand how our platform is used so we can optimize loading speeds and user workflows.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Session Cookies: These are temporary and expire once you close your browser. They are used primarily for authentication during your visit.

Persistent Cookies: These stay on your device for a set period. They help us remember you when you return to the site.

4. Third-Party Cookies

In some cases, we use trusted third-party services that may set cookies. For example, our analytics tools (like Google Analytics) help us understand platform usage trends in an anonymous, aggregated way to improve healthcare delivery in Sri Lanka.

5. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. However, please note that disabling cookies may affect your ability to use certain features of the PCOS Care Hub, such as secure login and symptom tracking.

To learn more about managing cookies on your browser, please visit your browser's official support website.
