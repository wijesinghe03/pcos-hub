Security & Trust

We use industry-leading security protocols to ensure your health data remains private, secure, and always under your control.

🛡️ Military-Grade 256-bit Encryption
🔒

SSL Encryption

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is protected by 256-bit SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) encryption, preventing unauthorized access during transit.

⚕️

HIPAA Compliant

We follow HIPAA-compliant data handling protocols, ensuring that your Protected Health Information (PHI) is managed with the highest level of confidentiality.

🇪🇺

GDPR Ready

We respect your "Right to be Forgotten" and "Right to Access." You have full control over your data, with the ability to download or delete it at any time.

🚪

Auto-Logout

To protect your privacy in shared environments, the PCOS Care Hub automatically logs you out after 15 minutes of inactivity.

🛡️

Data at Rest

Your health records and uploaded documents are encrypted while stored on our servers, ensuring they cannot be read even if physical storage is compromised.

👥

Access Control

No one can see your health data unless you explicitly connect with a hospital. You can revoke access to any provider instantly with one click.

Your Privacy is Our Priority

PCOS Care Hub is built on a foundation of trust. We never sell your data to third parties, and we use local Sri Lankan servers to ensure compliance with national healthcare regulations.
