Privacy Policy

Your health data is sacred. We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of every woman using our platform.

Last Updated: March 4, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to PCOS Care Hub. We recognize the sensitive nature of health information, particularly concerning Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our website and platform in Sri Lanka.

By using the PCOS Care Hub, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. Our platform is designed with "Privacy by Design" principles to ensure your health journey remains your own.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that allows us to provide a personalized health management experience:

  • Personal Identification: Name, email address, phone number, and date of birth.
  • Health Data: Menstrual cycle dates, symptom logs (acne, hair growth, fatigue, etc.), and lifestyle patterns.
  • Medical Records: Lab reports, ultrasound scans, and prescriptions that you choose to upload.
  • Hospital Connections: Data regarding your interactions with partner hospitals and clinics.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to improve your healthcare outcomes:

  • To provide and maintain the patient dashboard and tracking tools.
  • To allow you to securely share your health history with authorized medical professionals.
  • To send you appointment reminders and personalized health insights.
  • To facilitate hospital uploads of your lab results directly to your profile.
  • To aggregate anonymous data for PCOS research in Sri Lanka (no identifying information is ever used in research).

4. Data Security

Security is our top priority. We implement military-grade encryption and industry-standard protocols:

  • End-to-End Encryption: All sensitive health data and uploaded documents are encrypted during transit and at rest.
  • Role-Based Access: Only users authorized by you (e.g., your specific doctor) can view your health records.
  • Secure Servers: Our data is stored on secure, local servers that comply with Sri Lankan healthcare data regulations.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or lease our customer lists to third parties. We only share information with:

  • Authorized Healthcare Providers: Only the hospitals or clinics you explicitly "Connect" with on the platform.
  • Service Providers: Trustworthy partners who help us run the platform, all of whom are under strict confidentiality agreements.

6. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Access: You can download or view all your data at any time.
  • Correction: You can update inaccuracies in your profile or logs.
  • Deletion: You can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Revocation: You can disconnect from any hospital at any time to immediately stop their access to your data.
